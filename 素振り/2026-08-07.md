## Kamae

### Feet and Legs

- Both feet should face forward.
- The distance between the feet should be comfortable and allow you to push the body forward efficiently.
- Push with the ball of the left foot. Do not push mainly with the toes, which are weak, or with other parts of the foot, which generate less effective power.
- The right foot should not stick to the floor. Keep it light so that it can move forward easily.
- Keep the left leg straight so that power can be transferred efficiently toward the center of gravity. If the knee is bent or lockedhyperextension, it can interrupt the transfer of power.

### Center of Gravity 重心

- Your balance should feel comfortable and allow you to push the body forward easily.
- Imagine a vertical line running from the head, through the chest and abdomen, down to the floor between the left and right feet.
- Keep the center of gravity centered rather than shifting left, right, forward, or backward.
- center of gravity stability also includes keep pelvis stable to receive power and spread power to upper body.
- two side of the body should have a vertical line from ear to shoulder to pelvis to knee to foot

### Upper Body

- Keep the upper body fully relaxed. Excessive muscular tension can interrupt the transfer of power through the body.
- chest as resting naturally on the abdomen.
- head as resting naturally on the chest.

### Arms and Shinai

- Think of the left arm as an extension of the shinai.
- left arm and right arm naturally at two side of the body


### grip and tenouchi

- let shinai naturally sitting on the both hands' fingers
- Thenar eminence should be on top of the tsuga
- grip by pinky and ring figure with middle finger slightly grip
- no grip from thumb and index figure with block tenouchi
- bu still thum and index figure should naturally touch the tsuba for satety reason
- left and right hand should almost one fist away, but the reality is which way feels comfortable for swing and tenouchi
- no tight grip, otherwise the lower arm will have tense then upper arm, then shoulder ,which block the power kinect chain
- left fist one fist away from center of gravity


### distance

- keep i-soku-ito no maai, too close and too far will make the initiation hard, body need adjust to it.


## Cut

### initial move

- push left leg, let power spread to torso, then shoulder then left fist
- any tension in these musles will block the full kinect chain and waste power
- when left hand receive the power, add a bit additional power for swing up
- alwasy start from left hand, because it's the inner circle, right hand is the outer circle
    - if outer circle starts first, inner cicle cannot pass power to shinai, because the grip become diagnoal, less efficient to pass power, should be as much as possible in perpenditular angle for fist to tsuga
- the most efficient way to push shinai is starting from radius then to Thenar eminence
    the big arae just below the thumb.
- when torso receive the power, it immediately push right leg forward
- right knee moving first and let right feet follow it


### swing up

- using left leg power to initiate the swing, then add a bit power to swing up naturally,
- all move still in kamae form
- upper upper arm rotate in a vertical plane to the floor
- right upper arm also
- until the left fist height is good enough swing down
- need feed the eye-hand coordination

### swing down

- initiate by left fist first, using tenouchi, several different ways of tenouchi exist
- use right foot ball part to stamp on floor not heel, otherwise will injure heel.
- speed up the kensen first, make sure it has speed and inertia to run to target
- if initiate using right hand, left hand cannot add any power adn the tip will go to left of self.
- step the right foot, using the power to pull left leg, and using the power to push just tiny period of time of right fist to speed up the kensen, tenouchi

### after cut to zanshin

- afte hit the target, relax muscle
- keep the hand form just after cut and using footwork to move forward as fast as possible
- in moving keep the center of gravity almost at same height
- the knee coordinate the movement of foot, if using heel or foot ball part the height will up and down.
- catch up left foot all the right, otherwise will minize the speed.
- finish zanshin mind the person behind, and turn with kamae in mind.
- both feet should face forward, otherwise we cannot push using ball part.
