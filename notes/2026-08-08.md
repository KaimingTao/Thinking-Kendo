## How to think about the advices you get

- We receive advice every day—not only in kendo, but in all aspects of life.
- We may or may not understand it at first.
- Different people may give us conflicting advice.

- The goal is to find what works for us.
- The key is learning how to interpret advice: try it, observe the results, and evaluate it.
- If we don't try to understand and test advice, we won't know whether it is useful or how to apply it.
